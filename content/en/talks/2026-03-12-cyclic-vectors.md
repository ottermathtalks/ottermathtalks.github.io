The unilateral shift \(S \colon H^2 \to H^2\), \((Sf)(z) = z f(z)\), is the model operator of function-theoretic operator theory. This talk is a guided tour of its invariant subspaces via Beurling's theorem: every closed \(S\)-invariant subspace of \(H^2\) has the form \(\Theta H^2\) for an inner function \(\Theta\).

Along the way we compute the spectrum, checking that \(S\) has no eigenvalues while every point \(\lambda\) of the open unit disk is an eigenvalue of the adjoint,

$$ (S^*f)(z) = \frac{f(z) - f(0)}{z}, $$

and we finish with the question of which functions are cyclic for \(S\). No background beyond a first course in functional analysis is assumed.
