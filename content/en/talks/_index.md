Each month OTTER runs two kinds of talk:

- **The canon talk.** An invited expert gives a long-form, gentle introduction to a canon topic of operator theory or complex analysis. These are recorded and archived here, to keep teaching long after the live call ends.
- **The live session.** Two short talks of at most thirty minutes. The first speaker presents a paper or line of research that is *not* their own or a close collaborator's. The second slot belongs to the previous session's short-talk speaker, on a topic of their choosing — their own work included. The aim: a less myopic view of mathematics, and a speaker pipeline that renews itself.

October's relaunch session runs a single short talk; the two-talk relay begins in November.
