Quantum graphs are an operator space generalization of classical graphs that has been studied in operator systems theory, non-commutative topology and quantum information theory. This talk provides an introduction to quantum graphs and their applications in quantum information theory, then introduces a non-local game with quantum inputs and classical outputs that generalizes the graph homomorphism game for classical graphs. Based on joint work with Michael Brannan and Samuel Harris.
