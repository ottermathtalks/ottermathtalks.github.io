Professor William T. Ross speaks about his book *Operator Theory by Example*, written with Stephan Ramon Garcia and Javad Mashreghi and published by Oxford University Press. The book contains over 600 exercises and an extensive list of resources with detailed historical context — an invaluable resource for future generations entering the field, and for those guiding them.
