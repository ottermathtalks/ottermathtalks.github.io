In this talk, we utilize the tools of Clark theory to obtain spectral information about symmetric differential operators. First, we outline what a symmetric differential expression is. This leads into describing the self-adjoint extensions of a symmetric differential expression. Lastly, we compute (Clark) spectral measures for the self-adjoint extensions of specific examples of symmetric differential expressions.
