Let \(S\) be the unilateral shift on the Hardy space \(H^2\), that is, \((Sf)(z) = z f(z)\).

1. Show that \(S\) has no eigenvalues.
2. Show that every \(\lambda\) in the open unit disk \(\mathbb{D}\) is an eigenvalue of \(S^*\), with eigenvector the Szegő kernel
$$ k_\lambda(z) = \frac{1}{1 - \overline{\lambda}\, z}. $$
3. Conclude that the spectrum of \(S\) is the closed unit disk \(\overline{\mathbb{D}}\).
