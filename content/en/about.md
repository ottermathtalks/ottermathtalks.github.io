OTTER — **Operator Theory Talks for Early Researchers** — connects early-career researchers in operator theory and complex analysis: graduate students, postdocs, and visiting faculty. Function-theoretic operator theory is the main focus, though the scope is broad.

## Continuity

OTTER was founded in 2020 by Meredith Sargent, Douglas Pfeffer, Meric Augat, and Christopher Felder, and relaunched by a new organizing team in 2026 — same name, same mission, same structure. The full story, from a cancelled conference to a hundred-member mailing list in its first weeks, is on the [History](/history/) page.

## How it runs

OTTER runs on free-tier services, in-kind departmental support, and a few hours a week from its organizers — deliberately, so that it survives leadership handoffs. A new first- or second-year graduate student joins the organizing team each year. If you'd like to help, see [get involved](/get-involved/).
