---
title: "Get involved"
---

OTTER is community-run and zero-budget; everything below genuinely helps.

- **Give a talk** in the series — or recommend a graduate student or recent PhD who should.
- **Forward the September relaunch announcement** to early-career people you know.
- **Serve on the advisory board** — about two short calls a year.
- **Co-host an OTTER session** at a conference you're organizing.
- **Send news for OTTER News**: opportunities, open problems, community updates.
- **Share contacts in Mexico and Latin America** for the Spanish-language outreach.
- **Help identify funding paths**: departmental contributions, NSF conference grants, AMS programs.

Write to [ottermathtalks@gmail.com](mailto:ottermathtalks@gmail.com) — we answer.
