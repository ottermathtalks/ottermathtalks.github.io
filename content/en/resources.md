Curated for early-career researchers in operator theory and complex analysis. Some entries carry over from the original OTTER site; suggest additions at [ottermathtalks@gmail.com](mailto:ottermathtalks@gmail.com).

## Learning the field

- **Operator Theory by Example** — Garcia, Mashreghi & Ross (Oxford). The gentlest serious entry point; Bill Ross introduces it in [our archived talk](/talks/2023-04-04-ross/).
- **An Introduction to Operators on the Hardy–Hilbert Space** — Martínez-Avendaño & Rosenthal (Springer).
- **Pick Interpolation and Hilbert Function Spaces** — Agler & McCarthy (AMS). The community's core reference.
- **An Introduction to the Theory of Reproducing Kernel Hilbert Spaces** — Paulsen & Raghupathi (Cambridge).
- **Introduction to Model Spaces and their Operators** — Garcia, Mashreghi & Ross (Cambridge).
- **Bounded Analytic Functions** — Garnett (Springer).
- ["Dilation theory: a guided tour"](https://arxiv.org/abs/2002.05596) — Shalit's survey; pairs well with [Michael Hartz's archived talk](/talks/2021-11-30-hartz/).
- [Hilbert Spaces: A Sequel to Linear Algebra](https://rtullydo.github.io/hilbert/) — Ryan Tully-Doyle's free open textbook.
- [A Short History of Operator Theory](http://www.mathphysics.com/opthy/OpHistory.html) — quick historical orientation.

- arXiv new-paper feeds: [math.FA](https://arxiv.org/list/math.FA/recent) · [math.CV](https://arxiv.org/list/math.CV/recent) · [math.OA](https://arxiv.org/list/math.OA/recent) · [math.SP](https://arxiv.org/list/math.SP/recent)
- [researchseminars.org](https://researchseminars.org) — global calendar of online seminars, filterable by subject.
- [FOCAHA](https://sites.google.com/view/focaha/home) — Forum of Complex and Harmonic Analysis: events and community hub.
- [Fields Focus Program on Analytic Function Spaces](https://youtube.com/playlist?list=PLArBKNfJxuunyWLlzKfAadIlrKrtOF151) — recorded minicourses worth a full watch.

## Jobs & careers

- [MathJobs.org](https://www.mathjobs.org) — the main academic math job board.
- Broader nets: [AcademicJobsOnline](https://academicjobsonline.org) · [MAA Math Classifieds](https://www.mathclassifieds.org/) · [HERC](https://main.hercjobs.org/jobs/category/mathematics-statistics) · [HigherEdJobs](https://www.higheredjobs.com/faculty/search.cfm?JobCat=104)
- [NSF Mathematical Sciences Postdoctoral Research Fellowships](https://www.nsf.gov/funding/opportunities/msprf-mathematical-sciences-postdoctoral-research-fellowships) — the math postdoc fellowship.
- [AMS–Simons Travel Grants](https://www.ams.org/programs/travel-grants/AMS-SimonsTG) and [AMS Mathematics Research Communities](https://www.ams.org/programs/research-communities/mrc) — early-career funding and cohort programs.
- [A guide for mathematics graduate students (PDF)](https://math.dartmouth.edu/graduate-students/current/guide/GradGuide.pdf) — navigating grad school, from application to defense.
- [Project NExT](https://projectnext.maa.org) — MAA's professional development program for new faculty.

## Community & mentoring

- [Operator Algebras Mentor Network](https://oamentornetwork.wordpress.com) — structured mentoring in operator algebras and adjacent fields.
- [GOFIO](https://sites.google.com/view/gofiocaot/home) — young researchers in complex analysis and operator theory, mostly at Spanish universities, with a periodic online seminar plus grant and job pointers. A sibling community to OTTER.
- [SUBgroups](https://gradsubgroups.org/) — small online peer groups for first-year math grad students.
- [paraDIGMS](https://www.ams.org/education/paraDIGMS) — AMS initiative on diversity in graduate mathematical sciences.
- [MathOverflow](https://mathoverflow.net) · [AWM](https://awm-math.org) · [NAM](https://www.nam-math.org) · [Spectra](http://www.lgbtmath.org)

## Software

- [SageMath](https://doc.sagemath.org/html/en/tutorial/) and [CoCalc](https://cocalc.com/) — open-source computation, locally or in the browser.
- [GNU Octave](https://octave.org) — free MATLAB alternative.
- [NCAlgebra](https://www.math.ucsd.edu/~ncalg/) — noncommutative algebra in Mathematica; dear to free analysis.
- [QETLAB](http://www.qetlab.com/Main_Page) — quantum information toolbox for MATLAB.
- [A video introduction to Mathematica](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLd-yyEHYtIhKhXrzklfwupcbfe_LCcfoV) — by Ryan Tully-Doyle.
